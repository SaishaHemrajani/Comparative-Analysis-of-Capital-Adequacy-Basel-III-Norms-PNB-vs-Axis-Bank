# Comparative-Analysis-of-Capital-Adequacy-Basel-III-Norms-PNB-vs-Axis-Bank
A five-year comparative study of Punjab National Bank and Axis Bank (FY2021–22 to FY2025–26), analysing capital adequacy ratios, CET1, Tier I &amp; II capital, Risk-Weighted Assets (RWA), leverage and regulatory capital requirements under RBI's Basel III framework.
